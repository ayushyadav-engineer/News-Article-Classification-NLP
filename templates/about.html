About NLP

Turning language into learnable data

Natural Language Processing bridges the gap between how humans write and how machines compute. Raw text is noisy: it has punctuation, casing, plurals, and filler words. The preprocessing pipeline below strips that noise down to the words that actually carry meaning.

Dataset Information

AG News-style Dataset

The model is trained on a labeled CSV dataset of news headlines and descriptions in the same style as the popular AG News corpus, spanning four balanced categories.

World

International politics, conflicts & diplomacy

Sports

Matches, tournaments & athlete news

Business

Markets, companies & the economy

Sci/Tech

Research, gadgets & innovation

Machine Learning Model

Two models, benchmarked head-to-head

Both a Logistic Regression classifier and a Multinomial Naive Bayes classifier are trained on identical TF-IDF features. The model with the higher accuracy is automatically saved and used for predictions.
